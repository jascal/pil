=== WHOLE-MODEL (principled; no experiment needed) ===
  Every arch here has a final norm immediately before the unembed, which pins the readout ||r|| ~ const
  REGARDLESS of which weights are trained (frame-only or whole-model). So the normalized-margin distinction
  stays moot in both regimes; the only way to open the gate is to modify/remove the final norm. Whole-model
  has more DOF to widen the (raw) margin and to reshape retrieve/compose, at the cost of host fidelity.
