VERDICT: with training data held constant, certified-bit headroom does NOT follow a clean scaling
law (R²=0.27). It is high/noisy for tiny models (<400M: mean bstar_p90 11.4) and SATURATES past
~400M (410m..2.8b: mean 9.34, spread 0.76). => the PR #2 'Pythia improves with scale 13->9.4' was an
over-read of a narrow size range (70m..1b = the rising+knee region) on short text. The arch-vs-data
confound is resolved as NEITHER: it is a SATURATING CAPACITY effect (data fixed -> same saturation),
and Qwen's apparent 'flatness' is consistent because both Qwen points (0.5B,7B) sit in the >=400M
plateau. No universal scaling law (consistent with tau*). Gate stays CLOSED at every size (cv<=0.035).
