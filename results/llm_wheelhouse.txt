# PIC rule learner — LLM-wheelhouse benchmarks (2026-07-02)

All in the pythia-160m token space (pil.tokens.TokenSpace == HF tokenizers, verified).

## 1. IOI (indirect object identification) — the mech-interp canon task

"When A and B went to the store, B gave a drink to" -> A, real pythia tokens (29
single-token names), **test name pairs disjoint from training pairs** (memorizing
pair->answer transfers nothing). experiments/ioi_bench.py, results/ioi_bench.jsonl.

  PIL (hard, Datalog semantics)   0.966   (715 rules, 27.8k params, souffle exact 1.00,
                                           99.8% of decisions certified at δ=0.25)
  MLP (matched data)              1.000
  chance                          0.034

Notes (honest): the MLP *also* generalizes to unseen pairs — IOI's relational rule is
learnable in embedding space, so this task does not separate MLPs from rule learners the
way it separates both from pure memorizers. PIL needed equality literals (the Datalog
join x[o1]==x[o2]) and, critically, live structure search: with the solved-val gate
mistakenly frozen (val split shares train pairs) it plateaued at 0.73; unblocked it
reaches 0.966. The learned program is a relational+memorized mix (715 rules vs the ~58
of the pure "answer = the un-repeated name" program) — proposer-side compression open.

## 2. Pythia-160m head-to-head: learned vs decompiled vs host (the flagship)

Distill pythia-160m's argmax on 8-token windows (120k wikitext-2 windows labeled by the
model itself; semantics identical to rosetta's logit_cache keys) into a PIC program;
score against rosetta's decompiled circuits.dl via its own `souffle run.dl` harness on
identical contexts. experiments/pythia_headtohead.py, results/pythia_headtohead.json.

v1 (rank-64 lookup backbone only):

  arena                          | decompiled circuits.dl        | learned PIL      | bigram floor
  -------------------------------|-------------------------------|------------------|-------------
  wikitext held-out (20k)        | 0.040 overall (fires 12.6%,   | 0.322 overall    | 0.346
                                 |  0.318 where it fires)        | (fires 100%)     |
  home turf (300 cached ctxs)    | 1.000 (certificate reproduced)| 0.170            | --

The headline structure: **the two programs fail in opposite directions.** The decompiled
program is certified-exact on its mining domain and nearly silent off it; the learned
program answers everywhere at ~8x the decompiled overall transfer, and on contexts where
both speak they tie (0.318 vs 0.322). Precision-with-abstention vs coverage-without-
certificates — the claymore routing shape (decompiled expert first, learned fallback).

v2 added a full-rank direct bigram table (rosetta's gram2d shape, learnable, zero-init,
exported as lkpd facts) — and produced a clean negative: held-out DROPPED to 0.301 with
train agreement 0.93. An unregularized 50M-param table memorizes noisy (last-token ->
argmax) pairs where the count-based mode estimate is robust (results/pythia_headtohead.json
holds the v2 numbers). Fix (v3, in the ladder runs): initialize the direct table from
train co-occurrence counts — rosetta's gram mining used as an initializer (centered
smoothed log P(target | token)) — plus weight decay + reduced lr on that group, so SGD
refines a robust statistical estimate instead of memorizing from zero.

## 3. Pythia ladder distillation: behavioral distillability vs scale (7 rungs, complete)

Same 120k shared wikitext windows labeled by each teacher (argmax + top-8 logits);
identical program budget per rung (1536 rules, frozen count-init direct table, rank-64
lookups, soft+hard mixed distillation, val-slice death ablations).
experiments/pythia_ladder_distill.py -> results/pythia_ladder_distill.jsonl.

  teacher | PIL agree | count-table | lift/ct | bigram floor | teacher-top5 | certified
  --------|----------:|------------:|--------:|-------------:|-------------:|---------:
  14m     |     0.415 |       0.432 |  -0.017 |        0.436 |        0.664 |     0.70
  70m     |     0.311 |       0.343 |  -0.032 |        0.350 |        0.560 |     0.66
  160m    |     0.307 |       0.336 |  -0.029 |        0.342 |        0.529 |     0.66
  410m    |     0.296 |       0.326 |  -0.031 |        0.335 |        0.511 |     0.67
  1b      |     0.287 |       0.319 |  -0.032 |        0.329 |        0.501 |     0.67
  1.4b    |     0.277 |       0.308 |  -0.030 |        0.317 |        0.482 |     0.68
  2.8b    |     0.279 |       0.307 |  -0.028 |        0.317 |        0.485 |     0.66

Three findings, all crisp:

0. **The decline saturates.** From 1.4b to 2.8b (2x params) every column is flat to
   within noise (count-table 0.308 -> 0.307, top5 0.482 -> 0.485). The window-
   retrievable fraction asymptotes near ~0.31 — consistent with the earlier same-data
   pythia-ladder finding (results commit #3: headroom saturates, no scaling law).

1. **Distillability falls monotonically with teacher scale.** The retrievable-by-lookup
   fraction (count-table column) shrinks 0.432 -> 0.319 from 14m to 1b, and teacher-top5
   containment falls 0.664 -> 0.501, on identical contexts. Bigger pythias put
   systematically more of their next-token decisions beyond what 8-token window
   statistics recover — the learning-side echo of the analysis-side result that the
   composed/forge-tax fraction grows with capacity (tau_star_entropy: r_eff tracks
   capacity, not entropy).

2. **At this budget, learned rules add ~nothing over counting — uniformly.** Lift over
   the count table is a flat -2..-3 points at every rung (the residual trainable parts —
   frame, rank lookups, bias — trade a little argmax agreement for distribution fit even
   with the mixed objective). The program-distillable fraction ~= the count-retrievable
   fraction, at every scale tested. Honest negative, sharp interpretation: the teacher's
   non-lookup decisions on natural text need either relational/compositional machinery
   the current literal vocabulary cannot reach from ~90k samples, or attention. The
   companion measurement is the sibling `tropic` session's 2-layer *attention* student on
   the same teachers: the per-rung gap between its agreement and this table's is a direct
   empirical estimate of what attention buys (the forge tax measured from the learning
   side, between two student classes).

Method notes: three failed configurations preceded this one and are part of the record —
pure soft-CE trades argmax agreement for distribution fit (lift -0.055); an unregularized
full-rank table memorizes (train 0.93 / held 0.30); train-side death ablations keep
memorizing rules alive. Fixes: mixed objective, count-based init with the table frozen,
val-slice deaths. 1.4b/2.8b rungs pending label generation (CPU; GPU is shared).
