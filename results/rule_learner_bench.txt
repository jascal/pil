# PIC rule learner — benchmark results (2026-07-02)

All tasks are expressed in the **pythia/GPT-NeoX token space** (pil.tokens.TokenSpace reading
rosetta's bundle.tokenizer.json; encoder verified byte-exact vs HF tokenizers). "hard acc" is
the Boolean-gate (Datalog-semantics) accuracy — the number the exported Soufflé program
reproduces (verify_export agreement 1.00 on every exported program tested). MLP baseline:
embedding -> ReLU hidden -> logits, trained on identical data; params counted over used
embedding rows only.

## Headline table

task                         | eval                          | rules hard | MLP    | rule params | MLP params
-----------------------------|-------------------------------|-----------:|-------:|------------:|----------:
parity-8                     | unseen-pattern (n=4000)       |  **1.000** | 1.000  |         869 |     66,370
parity-16                    | strictly unseen (n=3287)      |  **0.993** | 0.976  |       6,239 |    131,906
induction (A B .. A -> B)    | held-out (n=1800, chance .125)|  **0.999** | 0.368  |       8,317 |     84,488
modadd (p=97, 50% of pairs)  | unseen pairs (n=4705)         |  **1.000** | 0.001  |       6,305 |     61,121
wikitext-2 (top-512 cands)   | val positions (n=8000)        |      0.248 | 0.236  |   1,072,358 |    846,432

- **parity**: AND-rules alone plateau at ~0.57; the PIC-T3 *threshold gate* (count >= θ,
  a Soufflé `count` aggregate) solves it. The learned parity program is the textbook
  depth-2 threshold circuit — head signs alternate with the ones-count parity
  (experiments/rule_program_demo.py prints it; results/parity8_learned.dl is the artifact,
  Soufflé-verified 1.00 on 1000 instances).
- **induction**: with only (offset, token) literals both arms stall (~0.40 rules / ~0.39 MLP);
  adding the *equality literal* `x[o1]==x[o2]` — the smallest instance of PIC-LP's
  position-indexed join, exported as `tok(I,o1,C), tok(I,o2,C)` — lifts the rule learner to
  0.999 while the MLP stays at 0.37. The learner recovers the induction-head clause that
  rosetta's decompiler finds in real transformers, this time *from data*.
- **modadd**: two-act story. *Ground rules only*: both arms memorize the 50% training table
  and transfer **0%** to unseen pairs (the grokking regime; an MLP needs orders more epochs
  + weight decay to grok) — the measured boundary of the ground-rule hypothesis class,
  mirroring PIC's composed/forge-tax residue. *With the schema channel* (ILP background
  knowledge: a modular-arithmetic + copy/pointer library; **selection learned** from data
  hit-rates, weights by SGD): the proposer adopts exactly `add_mod97[0,2]` (hit-rate 1.0),
  the pruner then deletes **every** ground rule, and the final program — one arithmetic
  Datalog clause over `num(token,value)` facts — scores **1.000** on unseen pairs in 6s of
  training. The copy schema in the same library is rosetta's `ind_ctxlogit` clause.

- **wikitext-2** (pythia tokenizer, top-512 candidates covering 0.60 of positions, 60k train
  windows): ground rules alone land *under* the bigram floor (0.197 vs 0.278) — anchored
  clauses cannot match lookup-table capacity. Adding **lookup source families** at the last
  two offsets (the rosetta ngram-backbone move; identity-gated sources `d = A_o[x[o]]`)
  lifts the program to **0.248, above the matched MLP (0.236)** but still under the bigram
  floor (0.278): the rank-48 factored table trades sharpness for shared structure (train acc
  0.77 — the gap is generalization, not capacity). Soft-hard agreement 0.990 here (LM scale;
  the sub-1.00 residue is hardening drift on near-tied decisions); PIC §5.5 certifies 0.795
  of val decisions robust at δ=0.25. Decision agreement with pythia-160m itself on the
  rosetta logit-cache contexts: 0.15 — reported as the comparability hook, not a claim.
  Scaling the LM program (more rules, higher-rank lookups, longer training) is open.

## Bridges (what makes these numbers PIC-native)

- train soft (log-semiring, T=1) -> harden (round_structure) -> refit heads: soft argmax ==
  Boolean argmax == Soufflé decide (decode temperature-invariance, PIC_SPEC §3.1, executed).
- per-rule ablation utilities are exact (additivity of the ⊗-monomial decode) — pruning and
  the utility signal are closed-form, not estimated.
- certified_fraction reports PIC §5.5: the fraction of decisions provably robust to
  per-token clause-weight drift δ (margin > 2δ) — the certificate for quantizing/pruning
  exported incidence tables.
