## VERDICT (problem #3 resolved, descriptively — to 72B)

1. τ* ≈ min(exp H, d) REFUTED, robustly to 72B. r_eff ANTI-correlates with exp(H) (Pearson −0.54) and
   POSITIVELY with capacity (hidden Spearman +0.63, nb/layers +0.58, params +0.52). Bigger, lower-entropy
   models use MORE-or-flat effective blocks, not fewer. The entropy law is wrong; not entropy-bound.

2. NO CLEAN FUNCTIONAL FORM — it is ARCHITECTURE-DEPENDENT.
   - Pythia (controlled): r_eff ≈ 0.4–0.5·nb, GROWS with depth/blocks (8→30 as nb 13→73). r_raw grows too
     (2.6→37).
   - Qwen: r_eff ≈ FLAT ~11–17, block-fraction SHRINKS 0.27→0.10 across 0.5B→72B (nb 49→161 but r_eff ~16).
     r_raw flat ~8–14.
   So the "≈12 scale-invariant effective blocks" reading was specifically a QWEN raw-PR artifact (Qwen
   r_raw flat 7.7–13.8 to 72B); Pythia's grows to 37. The two families genuinely differ in how decode
   block-usage scales — there is no universal r_eff = f(nb) or f(params, H).

3. WITHIN-MODEL: a modest POSITIVE entropy coupling survives (higher-entropy tokens use somewhat more
   blocks; Spearman ~0.2–0.47 in capable models, monotone entropy quartiles), ~0/negative only in the two
   tiniest (14m, 70m). The legitimate second-order grain of the τ* intuition — but a within-model effect,
   not the cross-model driver.

4. recon = 1.00 on all 14 (the residual decomposition is exact to 72B — the --pil-dump seam validated to
   72B). r_eff/e^H̄ climbs 0.07→1.74 across scale (r_eff up, e^H̄ down) — the clean refutation of "≈exp(H)".
